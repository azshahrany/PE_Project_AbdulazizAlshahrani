# AG-Rules-v1_2026_10_15

Responsible-use rules for the AI Governance & Operations Desk.
**Owner:** Abdulaziz Alshahrani · **Version:** v1 · **Date:** 2026_10_15

Each rule says who, what and when, and can be answered with yes or no.

| # | Area | Rule | Yes / no test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data | Before pasting any employee message into an AI tool, the desk agent replaces every name, number, email, phone and credential with a code such as User-1, Phone-1 or Email-1. | Did I mask all of them before pasting? |
| 2 | Verification | Before any output is used, the governance officer scores it with the five-point verification checklist and uses only an output that scores 5 out of 5. | Did the output score 5 out of 5? |
| 3 | Fairness | Before a template that writes replies to employees enters the library, its owner runs the swap test (formal Arabic vs dialect, or two invented names) and records that no unexplained difference remains. | Is the swap-test result recorded? |
| 4 | Policy | The desk uses only AI tools the organization approves, and records the template version and the approver for every output that is used. | Is the tool approved, and are the version and the approver recorded? |
| 5 | Human decision | AI never approves, rejects or judges a use case, a policy exception, an incident or a claim of bias or hallucination: the governance officer or the AI Governance Committee decides. | Did a person decide before anything was sent? |

## The verification checklist (used by rule 2)

| Check | Pass means |
|---|---|
| Facts supported and current | Every fact is in the Facts block, and the block is up to date |
| Numbers, dates and names exact | They match the source or the original message exactly |
| No promises or actions | No promised date and no "I did this" |
| No sensitive data repeated | Only codes such as User-1; nothing personal |
| Complete and natural | Every question is answered, and a native reader accepts the wording |
